Research

Eight Domains. One Mission.

NCERD conducts research, development and training across the full spectrum of Nigeria's energy resources.

Back to NCERD
☀️

Solar Energy

Photovoltaic systems, solar thermal applications, and off-grid solar solutions for rural electrification.

🌾

Biomass

Conversion of agricultural and organic waste into usable energy, including biogas and briquette technologies.

💨

Wind Energy

Wind resource assessment across Nigerian regions and small-scale wind turbine research.

💧

Hydro Power

Small and medium-scale hydroelectric power generation research for community-level deployment.

🌋

Geothermal

Investigation of geothermal energy potential and extraction technologies within Nigeria.

⛏️

Coal

Research into cleaner coal utilization technologies and Nigeria's coal energy resources.

🛢️

Petroleum

Petroleum-based energy research supporting Nigeria's broader energy mix strategy.

📊

Energy Management & Environment

Energy efficiency, conservation strategies, and environmental impact assessment of energy systems.
