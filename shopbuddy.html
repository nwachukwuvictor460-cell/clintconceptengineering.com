Why ShopBuddy?

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery within South eastern states usually takes 1-2 business days. Other states across Nigeria take 3-5 business days depending on location.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept debit/credit cards, bank transfers, and payment on delivery in select locations.

Can I return a product if I'm not satisfied?

Yes. We offer a 7-day return window for most items, as long as the product is unused and in its original packaging.

Do your gadgets come with a warranty?

Most gadgets come with a manufacturer warranty of 6-12 months, depending on the brand and product type.

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking link via email or SMS to monitor its progress.
