News & Events

What's Happening at NCERD

Stay up to date with our workshops, webinars, conferences, and research milestones.

Workshop

Solar PV & Biomass Training Workshop

Held at the NCERD Complex, Nsukka Campus. Hands-on training covering solar photovoltaic systems and biomass conversion techniques for students and industry professionals.

Webinar

Renewable Energy Solutions for Sub-Saharan Africa

An ECN-affiliated webinar bringing together academia, engineers, and energy experts to discuss SDG-7 aligned renewable energy pathways, hosted virtually via NCERD.

Conference

Energy Research Conference — NCERD Complex

An interdisciplinary gathering of scientists, engineers, and research scholars presenting on renewable and alternative energy technologies, with technical sessions and poster presentations.

Partnership

ECN Technical Advisory Collaboration

NCERD continues its role within the Energy Commission of Nigeria's Technical Advisory Committee, alongside sister centres at ABU Zaria, OAU Ile-Ife, and Usmanu Danfodiyo University.

Upcoming Short Course Enrollment

Enrollment details for upcoming short courses in renewable energy technologies will be announced here — check back for updates.

SDG-7 Aligned Research Initiatives

NCERD's ongoing research initiatives continue to focus on affordable, reliable, and cleaner energy access in line with Sustainable Development Goal 7.
