Programs

Training the Next Generation of Energy Experts

From short courses to full degree programmes, NCERD builds skilled manpower for Nigeria's renewable energy sector.

📘

Short Courses

Focused, practical training sessions on specific renewable energy topics such as solar PV installation, biomass conversion, and energy auditing — designed for working professionals.

🎓

Diploma Programmes

Structured diploma-level training in renewable energy technologies, run in collaboration with relevant academic departments of the University of Nigeria.

🏅

Degree Programmes

Degree-level pathways in energy studies, combining theoretical foundations with hands-on research experience at the NCERD complex.

Workshops & Conferences

Hands-On Learning Beyond the Classroom

NCERD regularly hosts workshops, training sessions, and conferences — including sessions on Solar PV and Biomass training — bringing together students, engineers, and industry stakeholders for practical, applied learning.

Recent Example

Solar PV & Biomass Training Workshop

Held at the NCERD Complex, Nsukka Campus — covering hands-on solar photovoltaic systems and biomass conversion techniques for participants across academia and industry.
