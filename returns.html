Returns & Refunds

Last updated: July 2026

Our promise

We want you to love your new gadget. If it doesn't work out, you have 7 days from delivery to return it for a refund or exchange.

What can I return?

  • Any gadget in its original condition, with all accessories and packaging.
  • Items that arrived damaged, defective, or wrong — free return pickup, full refund.

How to return

  1. Email returns@gadgetx.ng with your order number.
  2. We'll arrange a pickup or share a drop-off location.
  3. Once we receive and inspect the item, refunds are processed within 5 business days.

Warranty claims

All gadgets carry at least a 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects. Phones and consoles carry a 1-year warranty. Physical damage and water damage aren't covered.
