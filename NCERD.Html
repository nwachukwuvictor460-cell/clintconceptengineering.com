About Us

Nigeria's Premier Centre for Energy Research

Our History

Established 1980, Operational Since 1982

The National Centre for Energy Research and Development (NCERD) was established by an Act of the National Assembly in 1980, alongside sister centres including the Sokoto Energy Research Centre (SERC) at Usmanu Danfodiyo University and the National Centre for Energy Efficiency and Conservation (NCEEC) at the University of Lagos.

The Centre began full operations in 1982. The Federal Government later established the Energy Commission of Nigeria (ECN) via Decree No. 12 of 1988 and No. 19 of 1989, charging it with the legal mandate of supervising NCERD and its sister centres.

Key Milestones

1980 — Established by Act of the National Assembly

1982 — Commenced full operations at UNN Nsukka

1988–89 — ECN established and given supervisory mandate

Today — Active in research, training, and national energy policy support

Our Vision

To build a functional, globally comparable and competitive Centre for Energy Research and Development in Renewable and Alternative Energy Technologies, responsive to the energy and economic development challenges of Nigeria and beyond.

🧭

Our Mission

To carry out research, development, dissemination, and manpower training in renewable and non-renewable energy resources, and to mount short courses, diploma and degree programmes in collaboration with relevant academic departments of the University of Nigeria.

Institutional Structure

Where We Fit Nationally

NCERD operates under the technical supervision of the Energy Commission of Nigeria (ECN), Abuja — the apex federal body for energy sector planning and policy in Nigeria.

Host Institution

University of Nigeria, Nsukka — founded by Dr. Nnamdi Azikiwe in 1955, opened 1960.

Supervisory Body

Energy Commission of Nigeria (ECN) — established under Act No. 62 of 1979.

Sister Centres

SERC (Sokoto) and NCEEC (Lagos), among others under the ECN's Technical Advisory Committee.

NCERD

National Centre for Energy Research and Development, University of Nigeria, Nsukka Campus, Enugu State, Nigeria.
